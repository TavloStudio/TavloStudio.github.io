How to Rank on Page 1 of Google Without Spending a Penny on Ads

By Afolabi Oluwaseyi Christopher · 3 min read · Updated October 2026

Paid ads are great for immediate traffic — but SEO is what builds long-term, sustainable growth. Here's the exact framework we use to improve rankings steadily over time.

Step 1: Keyword Research That Actually Converts

Stop chasing high-volume vanity keywords. Instead, target "commercial intent" keywords — phrases people search when they're ready to buy. Tools like Ubersuggest (free) or Ahrefs (paid) make this easy.

Step 2: On-Page Optimisation

Every page on your site should be optimised for one primary keyword. This means it appears in your title tag, meta description, H1, first paragraph, and a few times naturally throughout the content. Don't stuff it — write for humans first.

Step 3: Create Content That Answers Real Questions

Google rewards content that genuinely helps people. Write in-depth blog posts, FAQs, and guides that answer the questions your ideal customers are actually searching for. This article is an example of that approach.

Step 4: Build Backlinks the Right Way

Backlinks (other websites linking to yours) are still one of Google's strongest ranking signals. Earn them by creating linkable assets — original research, comprehensive guides, or useful tools — and reaching out to relevant websites.

Step 5: Technical SEO Foundation

Make sure your site loads fast, is mobile-friendly, has a clean URL structure, and is properly indexed. Use Google Search Console (free) to monitor your site's health and fix any issues Google flags.

Consistent effort across all five areas is what separates businesses that rank from those that don't. The brands that win at SEO treat it as an ongoing investment, not a one-time task.
