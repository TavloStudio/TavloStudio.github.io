5 Shopify Store Mistakes That Are Costing You Sales (And How to Fix Them)

By Afolabi Oluwaseyi Christopher · 3 min read · Updated October 2026

Working on Shopify stores, I see the same costly mistakes repeated over and over again. The good news? Each one is fixable — often in a single afternoon.

1. Slow Page Speed

If your store takes more than 3 seconds to load, you risk losing visitors before they even see a single product. Fix this by compressing images (use WebP format), removing unnecessary apps, and enabling a CDN.

2. Poor Mobile Experience

Most e-commerce traffic now comes from mobile phones. Yet most store owners only design for desktop. Test every page on a real phone and simplify your checkout to as few steps as possible.

3. No Trust Signals

Shoppers are risk-averse. Add trust badges, real customer reviews, a clear returns policy, and a visible contact option. These small changes help shoppers feel safe buying from you.

4. Weak Product Descriptions

Don't just list features — sell the transformation. "This blender has a 1200W motor" is far less compelling than "Make restaurant-quality smoothies in under 60 seconds." Focus on benefits and outcomes.

5. Abandoning Cart Recovery

Many shoppers abandon their cart before paying. If you don't have an automated cart recovery email sequence (or SMS flow), you're leaving serious money behind. Set one up immediately — even a single reminder email can win back lost sales.
